# Undeify Website

The company website for undeify.com. Its job is to get Undeify through verification checks (Apple, Google Play, D-U-N-S, payment providers) and make a stranger trust us in 30 seconds. Repo: https://github.com/UndeifyLTD/CompanyWebsite (private). Isaac selected the local handoff prototype to replace the production interface; the precompiled React replacement is built and browser-tested locally, including a normal-flow cookie choice styled to match production, and pushed to the `isaac-revision` branch for review; it is not merged or deployed. Its [Formspree](https://formspree.io/) form ID and public submission flow remain unconfigured.
