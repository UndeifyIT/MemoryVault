# Undeify Website

The company website for undeify.com. Its job is to get Undeify through verification checks (Apple, Google Play, D-U-N-S, payment providers) and make a stranger trust us in 30 seconds. Repo: https://github.com/UndeifyLTD/CompanyWebsite (private, default branch `main`). A separate local prototype has a shared [Formspree](https://formspree.io/) contact form and an automatically looping facts strip, but the form ID and public submission flow are not configured.
