# YouTube Channel Audience Decision

The channel is made mainly for young builders in Africa; viewers elsewhere are welcome, but every episode is written for that person first.

- **Decided by:** Isaac, on 2026-10-30 ("mainly builders in Africa").
- **Context:** Junior's draft channel strategy offered two options: a global English-speaking audience, or builders in Nigeria and Africa first.
- **Options considered:** global (bigger but crowded, generic jokes); Nigeria only (sharpest identity, smaller reach); Africa first (chosen).
- **Why:** less crowded than global AI content, closer to Undeify's own market, and real local specifics (dollar billing, data costs, power cuts, foreign clients) make the "that's me" feeling the channel sells.
- **What it changes:** the channel strategy in the repo (`docs/channel-strategy.md`) now has Africa-first rules: laugh with the audience, never at it; continent-wide references by default; lead with free and cheap tools and their real cost. The next step includes a WhatsApp channel or community next to email. EP001's hook becomes "Africa's next unicorn".
- **Cost and risk:** none in money. Collecting emails or phone numbers is personal data under Nigeria's Data Protection Act and similar laws: consent and a privacy notice come before the first sign-up. Isaac decided the same day that the Builder keeps his concept look and skin tone and sounds American; the local feel comes from the scripts' specifics, not the character's accent.
- **Revisit when:** analytics after about 10 videos show the audience is mostly elsewhere.
