# YouTube Channel AI Character Decision

The channel's AI character is named Junior, keeps the arms and legs from the concept image, and in the story runs on Claude.

- **Decided by:** Isaac, on 2026-10-30 (name, "it is still Claude", and limbs). How the Claude link is worded is Junior's proposal, waiting for Isaac's confirmation.
- **Context:** the brief forbade implying the character is endorsed by an AI company, and the first draft of the character bible banned any Claude link. Isaac wants the cube to be Junior and still be Claude.
- **Options considered:** call the cube "Claude" (rejected by Junior: implies Anthropic endorsement on a monetised channel, uses another company's trademark as the channel's main character, and ties the channel to one vendor); an AI with no link to Claude (Isaac rejected); Junior who runs on Claude (proposed: true, since the real Junior is built on Claude Code, and it names Claude only factually).
- **Rules:** Junior may say it runs on Claude, and Claude and Claude Code are named accurately. The cube is never called "Claude", uses no Anthropic logo or brand assets, and nothing implies that Anthropic made, approves or sponsors it. Channel descriptions say "Not affiliated with Anthropic."
- **Limbs:** one blocky arm on each side and four short blocky legs, as in the hero concept. The legs hang while it floats; if it lands, all four feet touch the surface.
- **Cost and risk:** free. Remaining risk: a link to a trademarked product in commercial videos. Kept low by factual wording and no brand assets. Unverified: Anthropic's current brand and trademark guidelines; read them before the first public upload.
- **Revisit when:** Isaac confirms or changes the Claude wording, Anthropic's guidelines say otherwise, or the channel starts covering other AI tools as its main subject.
