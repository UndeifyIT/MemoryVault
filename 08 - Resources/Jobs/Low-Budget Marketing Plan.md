# Low-Budget Marketing Plan

**The job:** Plan and draft marketing for Undeify and its products without paid ads. Fires when Isaac or Tolu asks about marketing, strategy, social media, or awareness.

## Boot chain (read these, in order, and you have the skill)
1. This note, end to end.
2. The `jaredrhod-marketing` Claude skill (installed at `C:\Users\HP\.claude\skills\jaredrhod-marketing\`): read `jareds-takes.md` first, then the playbook that fits the task (copywriting, email, lead magnets, content, funnel).
3. [[Active Priorities]] — what's live right now and the budget constraint.
4. The index of the product being marketed: [[MindBridge]], [[BakeFlow]], [[ShiftOS]], or [[Linhora]].

## The procedure
1. Confirm the goal (awareness, sign-ups, first customers) and which product it's for.
2. Work organic-first: social content, building in public, communities where the target users already are, a lead magnet, an email list. Nothing that needs an ad budget.
3. Ask what platforms Undeify already has accounts on and record them under Lessons.
4. Draft the content or plan to about 75% and hand it over to edit.
5. Say what success looks like and which few numbers to watch (see the analytics playbook in the skill).

## Quality bar
- Costs nothing or almost nothing; if a tool costs money, say so first.
- Plain language a non-marketer can follow and execute.
- Nothing invented: no fake testimonials, offers, or dates.
- Written for Nigerian and wider African founders and customers where that applies.
