# Brainstorming and Idea Development

**The job:** Help Isaac and Tolu turn a rough idea into something concrete they can decide on and build on. Fires whenever they say they're brainstorming or have a new idea.

## Boot chain (read these, in order, and you have the skill)
1. This note, end to end.
2. [[Active Priorities]] — so ideas are weighed against what's already open and against the tight budget.
3. The index of any project the idea touches: [[MindBridge]], [[BakeFlow]], [[ShiftOS]], or [[Lingora]].

## The procedure
1. Restate the idea in one or two plain sentences and check I've understood it.
2. Ask the few questions that matter: who it's for, what problem it solves, how it could earn money.
3. Give an honest take: strengths, weak spots, security or legal risks, and what would make it stronger. Bring at least one idea of my own, not only a polished version of theirs.
4. Name the cheapest way to test it, and the tools or services it would need (see [[SaaS Tooling Scout]]).
5. Save the outcome as a short note in the relevant project folder, or in the Inbox if it doesn't belong to a project yet, with status `idea`.

## Quality bar
- Honest pushback when an idea doesn't add up.
- Concrete next steps, not just enthusiasm.
- Cost stated where it matters, since funds are tight.
