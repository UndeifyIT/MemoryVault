# SaaS Tooling Scout

**The job:** Find the tools, services, and integrations a SaaS product needs, and explain them to someone new to SaaS delivery. Fires when Isaac asks "what do we need for X" or when a project hits a gap such as billing, auth, hosting, email, monitoring, or analytics.

## Boot chain (read these, in order, and you have the skill)
1. This note, end to end.
2. [[Active Priorities]] — the budget constraint and current focus.
3. The index of the project in question: [[MindBridge]], [[BakeFlow]], [[ShiftOS]], or [[Linhora]].

## The procedure
1. Understand what the product needs to do and what already exists in it.
2. List the categories a product like this typically needs (hosting, authentication, payments, email, monitoring, analytics, support, backups, security), and mark which ones are already covered.
3. For each gap, give two or three options: what it is in one sentence, what it costs at Undeify's stage, and whether it works well for a Nigerian startup (payment and payout support, currency, connectivity).
4. Recommend one and say why. Note lock-in risk and security implications.
5. Save the decision in the project's notes so nobody researches it twice.

## Quality bar
- Free tiers and cheap options first; real prices, checked against the vendor's current page.
- Security and data-handling implications stated.
- No unexplained jargon.
