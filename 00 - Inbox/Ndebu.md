# Ndebu

A service marketplace where customers find and book trusted technicians and service providers (plumbers, cleaners, engineers and other skilled workers) to do work at their location. Idea stage; Isaac shared a founder roadmap for it on 2026-10-07.

- **Problem it solves and for whom:** finding a trustworthy skilled worker to come to your location. Customer group and location not recorded yet.
- **How it could earn money:** not decided. The roadmap lists commission, service fee, subscription and lead fee as options.
- **Cheapest way to test it:** not decided. The roadmap's stage 3 suggests interviews with customers and providers, a landing page and a waitlist.
- **Open questions:** first city or customer segment; how providers are verified; who carries the risk of poor or unsafe work; payments and refunds. Repo and co-owners not recorded.

## Founder roadmap

Isaac's "Ndebu — Founder & Startup Build Roadmap" (PDF, location not recorded) sets out 15 stages: problem and customer, market research, customer validation, business model, product strategy, MVP scope, brand and UX, technical planning, build with AI, testing and security, operations, legal and privacy, marketing, beta launch, and learn and iterate. It runs three tracks in parallel (business, product and technology, go-to-market) and has one rule: no production code until the problem, users, validation approach and MVP hypothesis are clear. Junior reviewed it against Lingora on 2026-10-07 and recommended adopting it as Undeify's standard project roadmap, with additions for decision gates and kill criteria.
