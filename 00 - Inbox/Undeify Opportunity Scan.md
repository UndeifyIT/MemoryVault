# Undeify Opportunity Scan

Findings of the 2026-10-05 search for the next software business Undeify can get paid for. Verdict: build nothing new yet; validate a workflow tool for Nigeria's licensed data-protection audit firms (DPCOs) through prepaid pilots, and test ShiftOS in USD as a side bet.

- **Full report (private artifact):** https://claude.ai/artifact/6QLHQBvbkTDJWYhgQPacT3. Source HTML and aggregate data: `research/` in the Lingora folder (local, not committed). Raw NDPC register files contain contact details and stay in the session scratchpad only.
- **Constraints given by Isaac:** sell to global USD customers, Nigerian businesses, or the rest of Africa; B2B SaaS, B2C or dev/security tools; 6–12 months to revenue is fine; $100–$500 to spend over 3 months.
- **Method:** 470 HN comments, 14,449 Reddit posts (25 subs), TrustMRR verified revenue, NDPC public registers, regulator and payment-provider docs. A critic agent attacked every claim over two rounds. Private Discord/WhatsApp groups were not accessed (needs consent).

## Key facts (checked 2026-10-05)
- Base rate: on TrustMRR, 67.5% of listed products earned under $1K all-time; 1% over $1M.
- NDPC register: ~8,500 registered organisations (UHL 2,304 / EHL 2,828 / OHL 3,366 rows), 364 DPCOs (266 active). The audit return (CAR) must be filed through a licensed DPCO; fees ₦100K–₦1M by tier; non-filing fine up to 2% of revenue or ₦10M (Templars, Jan 2026).
- Regulated finance is already largely registered (622 microfinance banks, 363 brokers). SMEs/schools/clinics are mostly OHL and file no CAR, so selling to them directly died.
- Vibe-app security scanners earn little (CheckVibe $11.6K, Vibe App Scanner $5.5K, SecureMyVibes $153 all-time). A web-search summary falsely claimed $2.2M for one: always verify primary pages.
- Payments: Polar's docs list Nigeria as a payout country (currency/fees/KYC unconfirmed); Dodo Payments closed to new Nigerian merchants since 23 Mar 2026; Stripe Atlas not needed yet.
- Idea A model (DPCO-only, unvalidated prices): ≈ $6K / $24K / $84K a year by month 24.

## Lesson
- Search-engine summaries can invent revenue numbers. Only cite TrustMRR, registers or primary docs, and record what was checked.
